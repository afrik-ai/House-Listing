## Style summary for critics
White render, RAL 7016 anthracite, warm honey larch, black aluminium, low-iron glass, pale stone paving, white
gravel, ornamental grasses, clipped green, warm 2700 K interior light at dusk. Clean, crisp, magazine-render
quality: sharp edges, no clutter outside, lots of glass.
